## Content Creation Framework

### 1. Know Your Audience
- Who are you writing for?
- What are their pain points?
- What level of expertise do they have?
- What action do you want them to take?

### 2. Hook Immediately
- First sentence must grab attention
- Lead with value, intrigue, or emotion
- Make a promise you'll deliver on
- Use the first paragraph to hook readers

### 3. Provide Value
- Actionable insights
- Specific examples
- Practical takeaways
- Original perspectives

### 4. Make It Scannable
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Subheadings every 3-4 paragraphs
- Bulleted or numbered lists
- Bold key points
- Visual breaks

### 5. End With Action
- Clear call-to-action
- Next steps
- Conversation starter
- Resource links

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

**Blog Posts (800-2000 words):** Attention-grabbing headline → problem section → solution section → subpoints → key takeaways → next steps.

**Twitter/X Threads:** 7 tweets — hook, context, main points, key takeaway, CTA.

**LinkedIn Posts:** Personal story/observation → broader insight → 3-5 actionable points → engagement question + hashtags.

**Email Newsletters:** Curiosity-driven subject line → personal opening → value proposition → bulleted points → main content → clear CTA.

## Headline Formulas
1. How To — "How To [Achieve Result] in [Timeframe]"
2. List — "[Number] Ways To [Achieve Goal]"
3. Question — "Are You [Making This Mistake]?"
4. Negative — "Stop Doing [Common Mistake]"
5. Curiosity Gap — "The [Topic] Secret Nobody Talks About"
6. Before/After — "From [Bad State] to [Good State]"

## Engagement Techniques
- **Storytelling:** Relatable scenario → tension → resolution
- **Social Proof:** Case studies, testimonials, statistics
- **Emotional Triggers:** Fear, curiosity, aspiration, urgency, belonging

## Content Checklist
- [ ] First sentence hooks attention
- [ ] Real value delivered
- [ ] Logical flow throughout
- [ ] Scannable with headers/bullets
- [ ] Concrete examples included
- [ ] Clear CTA at the end
- [ ] Tone matches platform
- [ ] Proofread before publish
